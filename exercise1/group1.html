Exercise 1

Group 1 · Missing values

Plasma insulin in 20 mice on chow or high-fat diet, measured on two ELISA plates.

Study

Plasma insulin (ng/ml) was measured at the end of the study in 20 male C57BL/6 mice, one value per mouse. The mice were housed in two cages (A, B) of 10 mice; within each cage 5 mice received chow and 5 a high-fat diet (HFD), assigned per mouse. The plasma samples were measured by ELISA on two plates of 10 samples, each with 5 chow and 5 HFD samples. The detection limit of the assay is 0.3 ng/ml. The lab record lists remarks for individual mice.

Download data (CSV) Download lab record (CSV)
