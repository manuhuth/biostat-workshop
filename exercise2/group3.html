Exercise 2

Group 3 · Covariates and model choice

Statistical modelling of the insulin data: two groups, covariates and model choice.

Study

The study of Exercise 1 is analysed further: plasma insulin (ng/ml) at the end of the study in 20 male C57BL/6 mice, housed in two cages (A, B) of 10 mice, 5 on chow and 5 on a high-fat diet (HFD) in each cage, measured by ELISA on two plates of 10 samples (5 chow and 5 HFD each). The data contain no missing values and no outliers. Each mouse has one insulin value, measured at week 12; the analyses can be done on the log2 scale or on the original scale. Start weight (g) and fasting glucose (mg/dl) were recorded at the start of the study (week 0), before the diets began; final weight (g) was recorded at week 12. Each row of the data is one mouse.

Download data (CSV)

For each question, choose the analysis, the plot and the numbers you consider suitable. In the presentation, explain why you chose them and give your answer. The page runs R in the browser; the first load takes some time. Outputs update when a selection changes. Exercises marked Sprinter are optional, for groups that finish early.

HYP = ["μ_HFD = μ_chow", "μ_HFD ≠ μ_chow (two-sided)", "μ_HFD > μ_chow (one-sided)", "x̄_HFD = x̄_chow", "x̄_HFD ≠ x̄_chow", "β_diet = 0", "β_diet ≠ 0", "β_diet = 0 at equal values of the other covariates", "insulin is normally distributed"]
