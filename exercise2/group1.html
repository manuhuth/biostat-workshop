Exercise 2

Group 1 · Paired design

Statistical modelling of the insulin data: two groups, covariates and repeated measurements.

Study

The study of Exercise 1 is analysed further: plasma insulin (ng/ml) at the end of the study in 20 male C57BL/6 mice, housed in two cages (A, B) of 10 mice, 5 on chow and 5 on a high-fat diet (HFD) in each cage, measured by ELISA on two plates of 10 samples (5 chow and 5 HFD each). The insulin data contain no missing values and no outliers. The insulin concentration of every mouse was also measured at the start of the study (week 0, baseline), before the diets began. Start weight and final weight (g) were recorded. Each row of the data is one mouse at one time point (week 0 or week 12); plate and final weight are recorded only for week 12 and are NA in the week 0 rows.

Download data (CSV)

For each question, choose the analysis, the plot and the numbers you consider suitable. In the presentation, explain why you chose them and give your answer.
