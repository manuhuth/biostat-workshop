Exercise 2

Group 2 · Nested design

Statistical modelling of the insulin data: two groups, covariates and repeated measurements.

Study

The study of Exercise 1 is analysed further: plasma insulin (ng/ml) at the end of the study in 20 male C57BL/6 mice, housed in two cages (A, B) of 10 mice, 5 on chow and 5 on a high-fat diet (HFD) in each cage, measured by ELISA on two plates of 10 samples (5 chow and 5 HFD each). The data contain no missing values and no outliers. Every end sample was measured in two wells of the ELISA plate (technical replicates). Start weight and final weight (g) were recorded. Each row of the data is one well.

Download data (CSV)

For each question, choose the analysis, the plot and the numbers you consider suitable. In the presentation, explain why you chose them and give your answer. The page runs R in the browser; the first load takes some time. Outputs update when a selection changes. Exercises marked Sprinter are optional, for groups that finish early.

HYP = ["μ_HFD = μ_chow", "μ_HFD ≠ μ_chow (two-sided)", "μ_HFD > μ_chow (one-sided)", "x̄_HFD = x̄_chow", "x̄_HFD ≠ x̄_chow", "β_diet = 0", "β_diet ≠ 0", "mean of the mouse means equal in both diets", "insulin is normally distributed"]
